New email signatures

Light, like the website and app. Big enough to read on a phone. Each one moves quietly.

What they replace

The current two, at the same width.

Jamie now: dark, small type, a 13.2 MB GIF that loads slowly on phones.

Gabrielle now: a static Canva graphic in a different style from everything else.

How it's built

  • Readable on phones. Name and title are in the image at a large size. Phone, email and website are real text you can tap, so they stay sharp and still work when images are off.
  • Fast. Each GIF is 0.9 to 1.3 MB, about ten times lighter than the current one, and loops smoothly.
  • Works everywhere. Gmail, Apple Mail and phone apps play the animation. Outlook on Windows shows the first frame, which is a clean still.
  • Dark mode. The card keeps its rounded corners, so it sits cleanly on a dark inbox.
